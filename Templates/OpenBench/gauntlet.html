The rating is the one at which the points expected against all of the opponents equal the points scored, which is the maximum likelihood rating given each opponent's rating on the list. Expected is the score that this rating predicts against an opponent, and Implied Rating is what the games against that opponent alone would say. The error covers the luck of the games. The spread is how far the opponents disagree beyond that, as their own ratings are estimates, and were measured at the time control and on the hardware of the list, not at ours.
