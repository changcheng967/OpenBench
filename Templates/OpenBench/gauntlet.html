The rating is the one at which the points expected against all of the opponents equal the points scored, given each opponent's rating on the list. The scale is how many points of the list one Elo point of ours is worth, and comes from the same games: lists are measured at their own time controls, where the gaps between engines are not the ones seen at ours. Expected is the score that the rating and the scale predict against an opponent, and Implied is the rating that the games against that opponent alone would give. Time and Crash count the games that either side lost on time or by crashing, and a red star marks an opponent with a crash or an illegal move: look at its games, and delete its test if they are not real chess, which takes it out of the rating. The spread is how far the opponents disagree beyond the luck of their games, as their own ratings are estimates too. The error includes it; From Games is the part that more games would shrink.
